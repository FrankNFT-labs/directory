We Are Dorkis is a collection of hand-drawn characters by Tori Batt, an artist from New Zealand. She drew more than 300 traits in pencil and ballpoint on paper, then scanned and scaled each one. The idea and the sale mechanics came from fccview. FrankNFT wrote the smart contract and a Python engine that layered the drawings into finished characters, because off-the-shelf generators could not handle paper texture or the transparent edges where one trait overlaps the next.

The contract went live on 31 August 2021, with the public sale at 0.07 ETH from 5 September. Ten thousand were planned. After a holder poll the team closed the sale early, on 19 September 2021, with 4,724 Dorkis minted. The planned burn-and-rebate only worked on a sellout, so it never triggered and no Dorki was ever burned.

The link to the punks is Psyborg. He held around 200 Dorkis and was also an original 2017 CryptoPunks claimer with 83 V1 punks he could not sell. In November 2021 he asked FrankNFT whether that could be fixed. The answer became the [CryptoPunks V1](/p/cryptopunks-v1) wrapper.
