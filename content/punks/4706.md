Software architect and developer from Belgium, and a keynote speaker in 10+ countries. Mined Bitcoin in 2010, came back for Ethereum in 2017, and again for NFTs in 2021.

Built the ERC-721 wrapper for CryptoPunks V1, the original contract Larva Labs deployed on 9 June 2017 and replaced two weeks later because of a bug in `buyPunk`. The wrapper went live on 17 January 2022 and made the V1 punks tradable again. It leaves the art alone: V1 and V2 point to the same image hash, and the lavender background was picked by a community vote.

Holds both the V1 and the V2 of punk #4706. As part of the MoonMars project, he sent that pair to the surface of the moon.

The wrapper came out of Dorkis. Frank co-founded We Are Dorkis in 2021 and wrote its contract and the Python engine that assembled its 300 hand-drawn traits. Psyborg, one of the biggest Dorkis holders, was an original 2017 claimer sitting on 83 V1 punks he could not sell, and in November 2021 he asked Frank to fix that. Six weeks after the wrapper he published ERC721F, a low-gas ERC-721 base contract. He wanted to call it ERC721B. The V1 crowd told him to call it F.

Co-founder of Hyperlabs, the blockchain brand of Xplore Group.
